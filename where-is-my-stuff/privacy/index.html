Where Is My Stuff

Privacy Policy

How AFLChaCoLi handles app records, exports, purchases, and support communication.

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy covers the features enabled in your installed version. Advertising provisions apply only to ad-enabled versions; publishing this policy does not enable ads in versions that do not include them.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Android app Where Is My Stuff, published by AFLChaCoLi. Privacy questions can be sent to aflchacoli@gmail.com.

The app does not provide an AFLChaCoLi account or developer-operated cloud-sync service.

2. Records stored on your device

The app processes and stores locations, items, ordinary notes, tags, importance, sensitive item text, sensitive location notes, image thumbnails, recycle-bin information, and app preferences on your device. Sensitive text is encrypted by the app on the device.

AFLChaCoLi does not operate a server that receives these app records, and the app does not use them for an AFLChaCoLi cloud-sync service.

2.1 Pro demo workspace

Versions that include Try Pro provide a separate on-device workspace with fictional sample records, without a developer account or purchase. Records, thumbnails, preferences and sensitive-area protection in this workspace are separate from your normal workspace. You can edit demo records and add your own content; the same local storage, encryption and export rules described in this policy apply. Sensitive sample records are created after you set your own local demo password. Biometric verification is optional and is handled by Android as described below.

The demo workspace does not display ads, initiate real purchases or perform developer purchase verification. Sample supporter badges do not represent purchases, and demo access does not change your actual purchase entitlements.

Confirming Exit demo, or expiry in versions with timed demos, clears the app-private demo records, thumbnails, preferences and local protection keys, without deleting normal records or changing actual purchase entitlements. Timed demos allow up to 30 minutes of cumulative foreground use per session, show the remaining time and warn before expiry. Backgrounding pauses the timer; closing or restarting does not reset the remaining time. Expiry stops demo operations and safely completes or cancels outstanding tasks before cleanup. Incomplete cleanup blocks reuse of that session and is retried when the demo is reopened. You can enter again with fresh samples after cleanup. Older untimed versions retain demo data until you explicitly exit. Exported backup and CSV files remain with the location or provider you selected and must be deleted separately.

New backups exported from timed demos contain an authenticated demo-session origin. They can only be restored in the same active, unexpired demo session, not into the normal workspace or another demo session. Normal-workspace backups and their supported legacy formats remain available for normal restore. Older unmarked demo backups cannot be identified retroactively. CSV exports are not encrypted or protected against copying; this feature does not prevent you from copying text or creating your own files. The local session identifier and usage time stay on the device and are not sent to advertising or purchase-verification services.

3. Backups and CSV exports

The app creates an encrypted .wimsbackup file or an unencrypted CSV file only when you start an export. Android's system file picker lets you choose where the file is written.

You may choose local storage or a third-party document provider, including a cloud-drive provider. If you choose a third-party provider, its service controls later storage, processing, or synchronization under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not automatically upload exported files to an AFLChaCoLi server.

CSV is unencrypted and may contain sensitive content only after an explicit choice and verification. Treat exported files according to their contents.

4. Google Play Billing

The app uses Google Play Billing to load localized product prices, process the one-time Pro and virtual support-badge purchases, acknowledge purchases, and restore ownership status. Google Play processes purchase and related technical information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

The app does not send your location names, item names, notes, tags, sensitive text, images, backups, CSV content, App password, or backup password as Google Play Billing parameters. Purchase tokens are used in the Billing processing flow and are not intentionally written to the app database, preferences, logs, backups, or CSV files.

4.1 Developer purchase verification

In versions with backend verification enabled, the app sends purchase tokens, product and purchase-option IDs, app/package version information, request identifiers and Google Play Integrity proofs over HTTPS to AFLChaCoLi's purchase-verification service hosted on Amazon Web Services. Later entitlement synchronization can use an opaque synchronization handle. The service checks purchases with Google, supports ownership restoration and refund/revocation reconciliation, and helps prevent abuse. These requests do not contain the organization records or passwords listed above, and do not create an AFLChaCoLi user account.

The service stores minimal entitlement states, verification timestamps and keyed pseudonymous references instead of raw purchase or Integrity tokens. The app may retain a minimal entitlement cache and synchronization handle on the device, outside its organization database, backups and CSV exports. Hosting providers process network information such as IP addresses as needed to deliver and secure requests. Verification data is not used for advertising targeting.

Minimal verification records are retained as needed for ownership, reconciliation and security, with expiry and cleanup rules. Where available, the in-app deletion action requests deletion of corresponding server verification records after proof of control; if the service is unavailable or the action fails, deletion has not been confirmed. Contact the privacy email for assistance without sending tokens or passwords. Deletion does not refund a Google Play purchase, delete Google's records or remove local organization records. A later restore or verification may create a new verification record.

5. Permissions and platform protections

When you choose biometric verification, biometric matching is performed by the Android system. The app receives only the verification result and does not read, receive, or store your biometric templates. Billing, enabled purchase verification, and enabled advertising/privacy-choice services use network access. The app does not request broad storage, device location, contacts, microphone, sensor, overlay, or boot permissions. Ad-enabled versions can include advertising-ID permissions supplied by the advertising SDK; IP-derived approximate location does not require device-location permission.

Android system backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app-private data. The app also uses screen-capture and recent-task protections. Platform and device implementations may affect the exact visual result of those protections.

6. Deletion and retention

You can edit or delete ordinary records in the app. If the recycle bin is enabled, deleted ordinary records remain there until restored, permanently deleted, or the recycle bin is emptied. Sensitive records do not enter the recycle bin and follow the app's permanent-deletion rules.

Resetting the sensitive area permanently removes protected sensitive data as described in the app's confirmation flow. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes app-private records from that installation, subject to Android and device behavior. Files you exported remain wherever you chose to save them and must be deleted there separately.

Because AFLChaCoLi does not receive app records on a developer server, AFLChaCoLi cannot retrieve or delete records stored only on your device or in a document provider you selected.

7. Website and support email

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. AFLChaCoLi does not add analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking scripts, or online forms to the site. GitHub may process web requests, technical information, and cookies under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact support by email, your email provider and Google's Gmail service process the message under their own terms. AFLChaCoLi uses the message to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, backup files, purchase tokens, financial details, or sensitive app records by email.

Support messages may be retained in the support mailbox only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to, troubleshoot, and document the request, or to meet legal, security, and abuse-prevention needs. You may request deletion of a support conversation by contacting the privacy email.

8. Children, changes, and external policies

The app is a general organization tool and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

This policy may be updated when app behavior, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is published.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • AWS Privacy Notice

9. Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-enabled free versions may display bottom banner ads on location, item, search and recycle-bin screens, including empty states. Unlocking the sensitive area does not by itself disable ads. Settings, purchase screens and the Pro demo workspace remain ad-free, and existing and future lifetime Pro users do not receive ads. Display remains subject to valid privacy choices, network availability, sufficient screen space and temporary interaction safeguards. Supporter badges are separate purchases and do not independently unlock Pro or remove ads. The first advertising integration does not include interstitial, app-open or rewarded ads, or third-party mediation adapters.

Ad-enabled versions use Google AdMob/Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on SDK behavior and valid privacy choices, these services process network and device/account identifiers, app or ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. We do not provide stored item or location names, notes, sensitive records, photos, backups, purchase tokens or order details as advertising-targeting parameters.

Where required, the app provides a privacy-choice form and an ad privacy options entry in Settings so you can manage choices again. Declining personalized advertising does not block local organization features and does not necessarily prevent all ads: available ad types depend on valid privacy signals and service configuration. When the consent SDK does not permit ad requests, the app does not request ads. Network failures or unavailable ads do not block local features.

When Pro takes effect, the app stops initiating ad loads and removes displayed banners. This does not mean every SDK component or privacy-choice network operation is absent, and does not automatically erase data already processed by Google. Google controls its own retention and deletion processes. You can manage your Android advertising ID in device settings. See how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's privacy controls and policy.

Privacy contact: aflchacoli@gmail.com

隐私政策

AFLChaCoLi 如何处理 App 记录、文件导出、购买和支持邮件。

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

本政策适用于你所安装版本中已启用的功能。广告条款仅适用于启用广告的版本；发布本政策不会使原本不含广告的版本自动启用广告。

1. 适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于由 AFLChaCoLi 发布的 Android App “东西放哪了”。隐私问题请发送至 aflchacoli@gmail.com。

本 App 不提供 AFLChaCoLi 账号或开发者自建云同步服务。

2. 保存在设备上的记录

App 在你的设备上处理和保存位置、条目、普通备注、标签、重要程度、敏感条目正文、敏感位置备注、图片缩略图、回收站信息和 App 偏好。敏感文字由 App 在设备上加密。

AFLChaCoLi 不运营用于接收这些 App 记录的服务器，App 也不会将这些记录用于 AFLChaCoLi 自有云同步。

2.1 Pro 体验空间

提供“体验 Pro”功能的版本会建立独立的本地空间及虚构样例记录，无需开发者账号或购买。该空间的记录、缩略图、偏好和敏感区保护与正常空间分开。你可以编辑体验记录及添加自己的内容；本政策所述的本地存储、加密和导出规则同样适用。敏感样例在你自行设置本地体验密码后生成。生物识别验证为可选项，由 Android 按下文所述方式处理。

体验空间不展示广告，不发起真实购买或开发者购买验证。样例支持徽章不代表真实购买，体验权限不会改变你的实际购买权益。

确认退出体验，或在具有限时体验的版本中到期，会清除应用私有的体验记录、缩略图、偏好及本地保护密钥，不删除正式记录、不改变真实购买权益。限时版本每轮累计前台使用最多 30 分钟，显示剩余时间并在到期前提醒；切到后台暂停计时，关闭或重启不会重置剩余时间。到期停止体验操作，并在安全完成或取消未完成任务后清理。清理未完成时不允许继续使用该会话，再次进入体验时重试清理；成功后可重新进入并创建新样例。旧的不限时版本在明确退出前会保留体验数据。已导出的备份和 CSV 文件仍保留在你选择的位置或文件提供方，需要自行另行删除。

限时体验中新导出的备份包含经过完整性验证的体验会话来源，只能在生成它的同一轮、尚未到期的体验中恢复，不能恢复到正式空间或另一轮体验。正式空间的备份和受支持的旧格式仍可正常恢复。旧的无来源标识体验备份无法追溯识别。CSV 未加密，也不具备防复制保护；该功能不能阻止你复制文字或自行制作文件。体验会话标识和使用时间只保存在本机，不发送给广告或购买验证服务。

3. 备份与 CSV 导出

只有在你主动发起导出时，App 才会创建加密的 .wimsbackup 文件或未加密的 CSV 文件，并通过 Android 系统文件选择器让你指定保存位置。

你可以选择本机存储，也可以选择云盘等第三方文档提供程序。选择第三方提供程序后，文件后续的保存、处理或同步由该服务按照其条款和隐私政策负责。App 不会自动将导出文件上传到 AFLChaCoLi 服务器。

CSV 未加密；只有在你主动选择并完成验证后，CSV 才可能包含敏感内容。请根据文件内容妥善保管。

4. Google Play Billing

App 使用 Google Play Billing 查询本地化商品价格、处理 Pro 和虚拟支持徽章的一次性购买、确认购买以及恢复所有权。购买和相关技术信息由 Google Play 按照 Google 自身条款和隐私政策处理。

App 不会把位置名称、条目名称、备注、标签、敏感文字、图片、备份、CSV、App 密码或备份密码作为 Google Play Billing 参数发送。purchase token 仅用于 Billing 处理流程，不会被 App 主动写入数据库、偏好、日志、备份或 CSV。

4.1 开发者购买验证

在启用后端验证的版本中，App 通过 HTTPS 将购买凭据（purchase token）、商品和购买选项 ID、应用包名及版本信息、请求标识和 Google Play Integrity 完整性证明发送至 AFLChaCoLi 托管于 Amazon Web Services 的购买验证服务。后续权益同步可使用不透明的同步句柄。服务向 Google 核验购买，用于恢复所有权、核对退款撤权及防止滥用。这些请求不包含上述整理记录或密码，也不会创建 AFLChaCoLi 用户账号。

服务保存最小权益状态、验证时间和经过密钥处理的假名化引用，而不是原始购买凭据或 Integrity token。App 可在设备上保存最小权益缓存及同步句柄，独立于整理数据库、备份和 CSV。托管服务商为传输和保护请求，会处理 IP 地址等网络信息。购买验证数据不用于广告定向。

最小验证记录按维护权益、核对和安全所需保留，并采用过期及清理规则。在相关功能可用时，App 内删除操作会在验证控制权后请求删除对应服务器验证记录；如果服务不可用或操作失败，不能视为删除已完成。可通过隐私邮箱寻求帮助，但不要发送凭据或密码。删除不会退款、删除 Google 的订单记录或删除本地整理记录；后续恢复购买或重新验证可能建立新的验证记录。

5. 权限与平台保护

当你选择生物识别验证时，生物特征匹配由 Android 系统处理。App 只接收验证结果，不读取、接收或保存你的生物特征模板。Billing、已启用的购买验证和广告/隐私选择服务需要网络连接。App 不请求全盘存储、设备位置、通讯录、麦克风、传感器、悬浮窗或开机启动权限。启用广告的版本可能包含广告 SDK 提供的广告 ID 权限；根据 IP 推断大致位置不需要设备定位权限。

App 私有数据的 Android 系统备份和设备转移备份已关闭。App 也使用屏幕捕获和最近任务保护；具体视觉效果可能受到 Android 和设备实现影响。

6. 删除与保留

你可以在 App 内编辑或删除普通记录。启用回收站时，已删除普通记录会保留到恢复、永久删除或清空回收站。敏感记录不进入回收站，并遵循 App 当前的永久删除规则。

重置敏感区会按照确认流程永久移除受保护的敏感数据。清除 App 数据或卸载 App 通常会移除该安装中的 App 私有记录，具体仍受 Android 和设备行为影响。已导出的文件会继续保存在你选择的位置，需要在对应位置单独删除。

由于 AFLChaCoLi 不在开发者服务器接收 App 记录，因此无法读取或删除只保存在你的设备或所选文档提供程序中的记录。

7. 网站与支持邮件

本 App 是通用整理工具，不用于主动收集儿童个人信息。

当 App 行为、法律要求或第三方服务发生变化时，本政策可能更新；发布修订版时会同步更新生效日期。

启用广告的免费版本可能在位置、条目、查找和回收站页面底部显示横幅广告，包括空内容状态。解锁敏感区本身不会关闭广告。设置、购买页面及 Pro 体验空间保持无广告，现有及未来的永久 Pro 用户均不显示广告。展示仍受有效隐私选择、网络可用性、屏幕空间及临时操作保护限制。支持徽章属于独立购买，不单独解锁 Pro 或移除广告。第一轮广告接入不包含插屏、开屏、激励广告或第三方广告聚合适配器。
