# AFLChaCoLi website

Official website, privacy policy, and support pages for AFLChaCoLi apps.

## Structure

The site uses static HTML and CSS and is built for GitHub Pages. It has no
client-side JavaScript or build-time dependencies.

Published content includes:

- the AFLChaCoLi home page;
- the Where Is My Stuff product page;
- the Where Is My Stuff privacy policy;
- the Where Is My Stuff support page;
- English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), and Traditional
  Chinese (Hong Kong).

## Public data boundary

AFLChaCoLi does not add analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking scripts, or
online forms to this website. GitHub may process web requests, technical
information, and cookies under its own privacy policy when the site is served
through GitHub Pages.

Where Is My Stuff stores app records on the user's device and does not send
locations, items, notes, tags, sensitive content, images, backups, or CSV
content to this website or to Google Play Billing. Google Play Billing handles
localized product prices, purchases, and purchase ownership under Google's
terms and privacy policy.
